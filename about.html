— Manifesto

We don't have a process deck. We have a habit: do the thing, show the thing, ship the thing.

Windikate is a four-person studio based out of Dehradun, with a sister office in Bengaluru. We started in 2021 making websites for friends. We're still mostly doing that — the friends are bigger now, and the websites have apps behind them.

We work end-to-end. Brand and identity, marketing sites, product design, the engineering. The same person who picks the typeface for the wordmark also writes the SQL behind the dashboard. We think this is how good work gets made.

We keep the team small on purpose. No producers, no account managers. If you hire us, you talk to the people who draw and the people who code — usually the same people. We answer email ourselves.

We also build a small line of products in-house — Windikate AI (a copilot for VC diligence), Apercept AI (behavioural simulation), and Win School (an LMS). They keep our skills honest in ways client work doesn't always.

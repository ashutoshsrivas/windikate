— How we work

Small team,
close work,
shipped quickly.

We keep the studio deliberately small — four people, no producers, no account managers in between. The same person draws and decides. Direct, fast, accountable.

Most engagements run six to twelve weeks. We open with a paid discovery sprint, ship visible work each week, and end with documented handoff. No surprises in the last invoice.

We do not bill hourly. We scope tightly, quote a flat number, and stand behind it. If something is off, we fix it on our dime.
